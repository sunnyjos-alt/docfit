Help

Learn how to use DocFit — step by step.

📷 How to resize a photo

  1. Scroll to Photo Resizer.
  2. Upload a photo (JPG, PNG, WEBP) — click, drag, or paste.
  3. Pick a preset (Passport, Government, Job, College) or enter custom width & height.
  4. Choose the background, format and max KB.
  5. Adjust brightness / contrast if needed.
  6. Click Process photo → Download, Copy, or Share.

✍️ How to resize a signature

  1. Scroll to Signature Resizer.
  2. Upload a clear photo of your signature.
  3. Pick a size preset (Small / Medium / Large) or set custom width & height.
  4. Set the maximum KB (usually 20–50).
  5. Leave Remove background off for best sharpness.
  6. Click Resize Signature → Download, Copy, or Share.

🖼️ How to convert, resize, compress, or crop an image

  1. Scroll to Image Tools.
  2. Upload the image.
  3. Set width / height (or use Percentage mode).
  4. Choose output format (JPG / PNG / WEBP) and quality.
  5. Optionally set a target max KB (e.g. 100).
  6. Click Process image → Download, Copy, or Share.

✂ How to crop an image

  1. Upload an image to Image Tools.
  2. Click Crop image….
  3. Drag on the original to draw a selection box.
  4. Drag inside the box to move it. Drag a corner or edge to resize.
  5. Click Apply crop when happy.
  6. Then click Process image to finish.

⚡ Quick presets

  1. At the top of Home, use the Quick Start chips.
  2. Click Passport photo → jumps to Photo Resizer with 350 × 350 preset.
  3. Click Signature 200×80 → jumps to Signature tool pre-filled.
  4. Click Compress to 100KB → jumps to Image Tools with target 100 KB.
  5. Click Convert to PNG → jumps to Image Tools with PNG output.
  6. Click Resize 50% → jumps to Image Tools in Percentage mode, 50%.

📁 How to save files to My Files

  1. After processing anything, go to History.
  2. Click the 💾 (Save) button on any entry.
  3. Choose a folder (Documents, Photos, Signatures, Certificates, Other, or your own).
  4. Click Save.
  5. Open My Files to see it — you can preview, rename, move or delete anytime.

⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts

  • Ctrl + V — paste an image from your clipboard
  • Ctrl + S — download the last processed result
  • Ctrl + Shift + C — copy the last result to clipboard
  • Ctrl + H — jump to History
  • Ctrl + 1 / 2 / 3 — jump to Photo / Signature / Image tool
  • Esc — reset the tool currently in view

❓ FAQ

Where are my images stored?

Entirely on your device. DocFit never uploads your photos anywhere — all processing happens in your browser.

Can I paste an image instead of uploading?

Yes. Copy any image to your clipboard, then press Ctrl + V (or paste on mobile) while a tool is on screen. The image is loaded instantly.

What is the Copy button for?

After processing, click Copy to put the result on your clipboard. You can then paste it directly into a form, WhatsApp, email or document — no file saving needed.

Why doesn't Copy work on my phone?

Clipboard access requires a secure (HTTPS) connection. On a local address it may not work — but it will work on the deployed HTTPS site.

How does DocFit remember my settings?

Your last-used width, height, KB and format are saved in your browser. Next time you open a tool, they're pre-filled.

Why does the download filename look odd (like docfit-photo-1727…)?

It includes a timestamp so files don't collide. You can rename it after downloading.

Can I use DocFit offline?

Yes — after the page loads once, all tools work without internet. Files stay on your device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. DocFit is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.
